⚡ 10-Second Elevator Pitch

"PodAI Live transforms static written articles into a dynamic, live audio podcast co-hosted by two AI personalities—Rachel and Drew. Unlike traditional text-to-speech where you just listen passively, PodAI lets listeners or judges interrupt in real time using voice or chat, get an instant factual answer from Host A, and resume the podcast seamlessly right from where they stopped."

🛠️ Tech Stack: Why & Where We Used It
  • Google Gemini 3.8 / 3 Flash: Why? Sub-second inference, 1M token context, natural banter. Where? In api/generate-podcast.js, api/ask-host.js, and server.js with a 4-model automated quota failover cascade.
  • ElevenLabs Conversational TTS: Why? Human-grade emotive prosody, breath pauses, and natural voice personas (Rachel & Drew). Where? In api/tts.js and server.js server proxy.
  • Web Speech API (Synthesis & Recognition): Why? 0ms offline fallback for campus Wi-Fi + instant mic voice input without third-party audio latency. Where? In js/app.js:speakTextRobust & toggleMicListen.
  • Vercel Global Edge & Serverless: Why? Production cloud hosting, automated SSL, protecting secret API keys. Where? In api/ endpoints & vercel.json.
  • Node.js Local Server: Why? Complies with Hackathon Rule #4 (Live deployment preferred but not mandatory) ensuring 100% immune presentation performance. Where? In server.js on http://localhost:3000.
  • Modern Glassmorphic HTML5/CSS3: Why? Futuristic UI, glowing equalizers, zero framework bloat (0ms load time). Where? In index.html and css/style.css.

🎯 Top Judge Questions & Winning Answers
Q1: How is this different from Google NotebookLM?
Answer: NotebookLM creates static audio files you must passively sit through. PodAI is a live, interactive conversation: you can pause, interrupt with your voice, get clarifying answers on confusing terms, and resume seamlessly.
Q2: How does Host A know the context of where the podcast was paused?
Answer: The client captures the exact sentence index, current speaker, and surrounding discussion, feeding it to Gemini with prompt rules: sentence 1 gives the factual definition, sentence 2 ties it back to the show.
Q3: How do you handle audio latency when someone interrupts?
Answer: We engineered a Non-Blocking Promise Resolver. The instant Pause or a Question is triggered, all ongoing streams cancel within 10ms without page reloads.
Q4: Why dual-host instead of a single voice narrator?
Answer: Cognitive studies show retention increases over 40% with dialogue. Host A is the curious guide; Host B is the analytical expert. The back-and-forth keeps listeners engaged.
Q5: What is your business model?
Answer: B2B licensing for newsletter platforms (Substack, Beehiiv) to offer interactive audio editions to subscribers, and enterprise onboarding to convert dense technical wikis into interactive podcasts.
